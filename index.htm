Fees come from REDOI trades. Investment income comes from portfolio assets.

How reinvestment works

We return part of the investment income to the portfolio. If those investments generate more income, it can be reinvested again. This is how compounding works. The DAO can change how income is allocated to support the token's development and announces its decisions.

Payouts in TON

To receive payouts in TON, simply hold REDOI in your own wallet. When the smart contract accumulates enough funds, it automatically sends payouts to token holders. You do not need to claim them.

Questions

Does treasury growth mean an equal increase in the value of my REDOI?

There is no direct relationship. Treasury value, the token's market price, and payouts to token holders are separate measures. A token holder's financial result depends on payouts, purchase and sale prices, fees, and market conditions.

What part of my purchase goes to the treasury?

When you buy REDOI, the funds go to the seller or liquidity pool. The smart contract distributes the fee, and 40% of that fee goes to the project's investment portfolio.

Who runs the project?

The REDOI team manages investment work. The owner holds a substantial share of the token supply. We publish DAO decisions and project news in the channel.
